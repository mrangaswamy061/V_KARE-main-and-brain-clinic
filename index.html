Frequently Asked Questions

How does online counselling work?
Once you book a slot and complete the checkout, a secure meeting room is unlocked in your Patient Dashboard. Simply log in and click "Join Meeting" at the scheduled time.
Is my information safe?
Yes, we implement bank-level AES-256 encryption for data. Files uploaded are scanned, renamed, and stored securely. Doctors only have access to their assigned patients.
Can I reschedule or cancel?
Absolutely. You can reschedule or cancel any appointment from your dashboard up to 2 hours before the start time.
